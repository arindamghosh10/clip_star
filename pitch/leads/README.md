## Change one line of the email per group

- **Clipping agencies:** "You sell clips by the episode. clip_star cuts, captions and schedules 30 clips per episode in minutes, so each client costs you less to serve."
- **Podcast agencies:** "Add a short-form tier for every client show without hiring another editor."
- **Church agencies:** "Every Sunday sermon becomes 20 captioned clips, posted on each church's accounts, with one approval click."
- **Marketplaces (partner):** "Your brands and top clippers need clips cut fast. Let's talk about an integration."
- **Big creator media (cold):** "Self-hosted, so your footage and data never leave your servers."

## Finding a real email, quickly and legitimately

1. Check the company's contact or about page (footer emails like hello@, partnerships@).
2. Use the "Book a call" or contact form. Paste the short email there.
3. Message the founder on LinkedIn or X with the DM version from `../EMAIL.md`.
4. For agencies with a Clutch profile, use "Contact" on Clutch.
5. Look up a person's email in a finder tool (Hunter, Apollo) only if it shows a verified result. Don't send to guessed addresses: bounces hurt your sender reputation.

## Two warnings

- **Clipstar (Ottawa)** is a clipping agency with nearly the same name as your product. Check for trademark conflicts before you send a lot of email.
- **Many clipping agencies distribute through thousands of clippers' own accounts.** clip_star automates editing and posting to accounts *you* connect. Pitch them on the production side (editing, captions, their own pages, reporting), not on running their clipper network.
